## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

### When generating in ElevenLabs Video:

1. **Generate 3 takes per prompt** — pick the best one
2. **Lock the style** between generations by referring to "the same lab as the previous shot" or "same aesthetic as segment 5"
3. **Avoid faces** in close-up — they're the hardest thing for AI to render convincingly. Hands, equipment, environments work great.
4. **Reject any clip** with:
   - Text overlays that weren't requested
   - Warm yellow/orange tones (we're cool blue)
   - Cartoon or unrealistic styles
   - Multiple people doing nothing
5. **Keep clips 3-8 seconds** — long enough to register, short enough not to bore

### If ElevenLabs limits B-roll duration:
- Generate priority segments first: 1, 3, 5-8 (the foundational shots)
- For lower-priority segments (10-19), I can fall back to my Manim animations
- Or use Pexels/Pixabay free stock for filler
