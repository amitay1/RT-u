[matter-of-fact] Wrong timebase. Every measurement after this is wrong.

Let's set it correctly.

[confident] Welcome to MCI/O Training. In four and a half minutes you'll pick the right units, set range and delay, choose absolute or relative mode, and lock the sampling frequency.

All from the Timebase tab.

[serious] Before you touch any timebase parameter, the manual is explicit.

Zoom delay and zoom range must both be at their minimum values.

If you skip this, your range and delay adjustments behave unpredictably.

The Timebase axis can be shown in five units.

Samples — short, Smpls. The axis indicates the number of samples in the A-scan waveform.

Time — microseconds.

Depth. Timebase units are set based on the material velocity of the material being inspected.

Vertical depth, or Vdpth. Used for shear or longitudinal wave inspections at non-zero refraction angles. The timebase is calculated from velocity, time, and the cosine of the beam angle.

Horizontal depth, or Hdpth. Same wave types, but used when you want the distance from the transducer to the indication.

To pick units, lower-middle of the Timebase tab, dropdown under Units.

Three values you adjust manually. Range, Delay, Offset.

Each click of the combo boxes changes the value by an increment you control.

Delay. Distance from the timebase trigger to the start of your range.

Range. The visible window after delay.

Both display their minimum and maximum permitted values at the bottom of their wheels.

Offset. Shifts the zero of the timebase units, negative or positive.

Two modes for the timebase itself.

Absolute — Abs — the range begins after the delay. The leftmost unit is the delay value.

Relative — Rel — the range includes the delay. The leftmost unit is zero.

Lower-left of the tab, under Mode, pick the radio button you want.

Sampling frequency is the number of samples captured in one P-R-F cycle. Expressed in mega-samples per second — M-S-P-S.

The rule from the manual: sampling frequency should be at least four times the probe centre frequency.

Default and recommended value: one hundred.

Middle of the Timebase tab, under MSPS, pick from the dropdown or type a value.

The big mistake. Adjusting timebase parameters while zoom delay or zoom range are above minimum.

The manual is explicit about this. Both must be at minimum. Always.

Quick recap.

Always minimise zoom delay and zoom range first.

Five unit options: Samples, Time, Depth, Vertical depth, Horizontal depth.

Adjust Range, Delay, Offset with increment-controlled combo boxes or wheels.

Pick Absolute or Relative mode.

Sampling frequency: at least four times probe centre frequency. Default one hundred.
