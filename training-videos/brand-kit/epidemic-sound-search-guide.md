# 🎵 Epidemic Sound — Search Strategy

How to find the exact tracks we need without spending hours scrolling. Each video gets one main track + an optional B-section.

---

## 🔑 Universal Search Filters (always set)

When you open the Epidemic Sound search:

| Filter | Value | Why |
|--------|-------|-----|
| **Mood** | Hopeful, Confident, Focused | Avoid melancholic, dark, intense |
| **Energy** | 4-7 / 10 | Not too sleepy, not too aggressive |
| **BPM** | 90-130 | Steady technical pace |
| **Instruments** | Piano, Strings, Synths, Pads | NO drums-only tracks |
| **Vocals** | None | We have voiceover already |
| **Length** | ≥ 3:00 | Need room to fade in/out |

## ⚙️ Track Settings in Descript

When you drop an Epidemic track onto Descript's timeline:

1. Set the music track volume to **-28 LUFS** (Descript shows dB — aim for about -18 dB below the VO).
2. Enable **Auto-Duck** under voiceover — Descript ducks music by 6 dB when VO is speaking.
3. Add a **2-second fade-in** at the start and a **3-second fade-out** at the end.
4. For most videos, music starts at **0:05** (after the cold open silence) and ends at the outro sting.
