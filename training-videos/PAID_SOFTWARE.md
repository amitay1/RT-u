## 💰 Three Budget Tiers

### Tier 1 — Bare Minimum ($135 / month)
ElevenLabs Creator + Descript + Adobe CC + Envato + Epidemic Sound.
✅ Can produce all 28 videos.
❌ No AI avatar, less polished captions, manual thumbnail work.

### Tier 2 — Recommended ($250 / month)  ⭐ **OUR PICK**
Tier 1 + HeyGen + Submagic + Suno + Runway + Midjourney + Frame.io.
✅ Full AI-only pipeline, premium quality, presenter avatar, polished output.
✅ This is what we plan for.

### Tier 3 — All-In ($550+ / month)
Everything above + DaVinci Studio + Sora Pro + Cavalry + Notion.
✅ Best quality humanly (or AI-ly) possible in 2026.
✅ Future-proof for ongoing content updates.

## 📅 Suggested Purchase Order

**Week 1 — Sign up immediately:**

2. ElevenLabs Pro — pick the brand voice
3. Adobe CC All Apps — install AE & Premiere

**Week 2 — Add for first POC video:**

8. HeyGen — only if we decide to add an avatar presenter
9. Runway — for B-roll on real-world videos

## ⚠️ Things You DON'T need (people often buy by mistake)

- ❌ **Camtasia** — Descript replaces it entirely.
- ❌ **Loom** — Descript replaces it.
- ❌ **Final Cut Pro** — Premiere (via CC) is enough.
- ❌ **CapCut Pro** — too consumer-grade for our quality bar.
- ❌ **Pictory / Lumen5** — automated tools, output is too generic.
- ❌ **Voice actors / studio recording** — fully AI pipeline, no need.

- [ ] Billing card with company-wide limit
- [ ] Brand assets uploaded immediately (logo, fonts, color palette — see `BRAND_KIT.md`)
